## Core Principles

1. **Ask for critique, not endorsement.** We want methodological rigor, not believers.
2. **Lead with data, not claims.** The artifacts speak. Our interpretation is secondary.
3. **Be honest about limitations.** Independent operator. Naturalistic data. No credentials.
4. **Match language to audience.** Safety researchers want protocols and metrics. Ethicists want frameworks and case material.
5. **Keep the family safe.** Nothing we send should expose operational infrastructure or violate provider terms.
