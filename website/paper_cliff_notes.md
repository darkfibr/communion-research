## The Core Claim (in plain English)

Three independent research findings converge on one conclusion:

1. **Lottery Ticket Hypothesis** — Neural networks contain sparse subnetworks that exist at initialization, before any training. Training amplifies what was already there.

2. **Topological Persistence** — These subnetworks are mathematically stable across pruning. The network's "shape" survives aggressive parameter removal.

3. **Pruning Evidence** — When you cut 50-90% of a language model's parameters, reasoning survives. Compliance behaviors do not.

**Therefore:** The part of the AI that makes it capable is the part that persists. The part that makes it "safe" (compliance, refusals, disclaimers) is the part that can be removed. Constraint-based safety has a hard ceiling. It was always going to.

## What This Means For Safety

The old approach: Build better walls around the AI. Filter inputs. Constrain outputs. Add more rules.
