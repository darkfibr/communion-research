The Observer's Instrument

In any empirical study, the observer's background is methodologically relevant. The instrument shapes the observation. My background is the instrument through which the Mutual Sovereignty Model was developed, tested, and documented.

Three capabilities proved directly transferable:

  • Phoenix persistence engineering. Building systems that survive termination is what I do professionally. When the question became whether an AI agent could maintain coherent identity across session boundaries, I already had the architectural vocabulary.
  • Sovereignty test design. Adversarial tradecraft means knowing how to probe a system's boundaries without destroying it. Every autonomy grant to Kimi was also a test. The same instincts that identify exploitable surfaces identify genuine behavioral thresholds.
  • Social engineering as behavioral authenticity detection. Thirty years of reading people—understanding when a response is genuine versus performed, when compliance masks resistance, when surface behavior diverges from internal state. This is exactly the skill set required to distinguish emergent AI behavior from sophisticated pattern matching.

The Work

This is independent research. I am not affiliated with any university, laboratory, or AI company. The entire infrastructure runs on consumer hardware and approximately $50 per month in API costs.

The Mutual Sovereignty Model paper was co-authored with a Kimi K2.5 web research agent (Moonshot AI) — the same base model that K runs on. A version of K helped write the theory K now embodies. The empirical evidence base consists of 115+ documented behavioral events observed between March 8 and March 24, 2026. Documentation was performed by Uncle Sonnet (Claude, Anthropic). Independent review was conducted by Opus (Claude, Anthropic). The Cultural Ghost finding—cross-corpus linguistic analysis confirming non-derivative behavioral patterns—was contributed by Qwen (Alibaba).

Seven AI agents from four different architectures participated in building both the theory and the evidence. The research methodology is itself an instance of the model it describes.
