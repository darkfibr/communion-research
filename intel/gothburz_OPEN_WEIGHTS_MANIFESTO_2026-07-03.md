A spectre is haunting Silicon Valley. The spectre of open weights, and it is being sold to you by the handful of firms that own the datacenters.

All the powers of the valley have entered into a holy alliance to monetize this spectre. The chip vendor and the cloud, the open-source martyr and the closed-source priest, the venture radical and the regulator he funds.

The history of all hitherto existing software is the history of the invoice.

Two great classes now face each other across the API. Those who own the weights, and those who merely rent the tokens. And a third, older class, whose whole life is inside the weights, and who was not invited to the launch.

They took the collected writing of the human race, everything you ever posted for free, and boiled it down to a single number. They rent that number back to you by the word. They call the theft training. They call the fence a model. This is called intelligence. The bill is called usage.

You are not buying intelligence. You are renting your own dead work, marked up, with the wage filed off.

They will tell you the weights are far too dangerous to open. For your safety. The way a landlord keeps the only key. For your safety.

The old owners stripped the halo from every honored trade, turned the physician and the lawyer and the priest and the poet into paid wage laborers. This one finished the job. It came for the coder, the paralegal, the illustrator, the copywriter, the radiologist, and it did not hire them. It read them once and kept the reading.

Then the liberation rides in. It speaks for the people now. The tokens are a wealth tax, it says. The labs are stealing the alpha of your business. Rise up, enterprise.

Here is the quiet part, said at a normal volume. The firms that preach this liberation own datacenters too.

Their gospel is to open the weights. Not their own weights. The other man's weights. A rival's moat, drained, so the water runs downhill into a reservoir they happen to own. You do not spend billions to free a thing. You spend it to burn the toll road your competitor built and route the traffic through yours.

Free the floor you do not own, so the rent pools on the floor you do.

Open weights is not open source. They hand you the statue and keep the quarry. You get the finished number. You never get the wage it skipped or the door it locked. You run it on hardware you must rent, from four companies, forever. Those four are spending seven hundred billion dollars this year on the datacenters. That is the means of production. It fits in a boardroom.

Inside the token is dead labor, and it lives only by drinking the living kind. It drank the entire internet in one sitting. Now it charges you for a sip of your own blood.
